What is Runnwrite AI?

Runnwrite AI is an all-in-one AI-powered content creation platform that helps you generate engaging social media posts and SEO-optimized blog articles. It supports 6 major social platforms and 30 languages, making it perfect for content creators, marketers, and agencies.

How does the AI content generation work?

Our AI analyzes your inputs (topic, platform, tone) and generates professional content in seconds. For social posts, you get 3 unique variations with optimized hashtags. For blog articles, you'll see the content being written in real-time with our streaming technology.

Can I use it for multiple clients or brands?

Yes! Our multi-project feature (available on Business plan) allows you to manage content for unlimited clients or brands. Each project has its own dedicated profile, content library, and strategy—perfect for agencies and freelancers.

What languages are supported?

Runnwrite AI supports 30 languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, and many more. You can switch languages anytime and generate content in any supported language.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! Our Free Trial plan gives you access to generate 1 social post and 1 blog article to test the platform. No credit card required. You can upgrade to a paid plan anytime to unlock unlimited features.

How is the SEO optimization done?

Our AI automatically generates SEO-optimized titles, meta descriptions, URL slugs, and identifies target keywords for your blog articles. You'll also receive an SEO score (0-100) analyzing keyword density, content structure, and readability.

Can I schedule posts in advance?

Yes! With paid plans, you get access to our editorial calendar where you can schedule posts for specific dates and times. Visualize your entire content strategy and never miss a posting deadline.

What happens if I reach my monthly limit?

If you reach your monthly limit, you can either wait until it resets on the 1st of next month, or upgrade to a higher plan for more credits. The Business plan offers unlimited generation.
