Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our AI content generator help and content generator support

How does the AI content generator work?

Our AI content generator uses advanced natural language processing to create high-quality content. Simply choose your content type (social media post or blog article), enter your topic or keywords, and our AI writing tool generates optimized content tailored to your needs. The process takes less than 60 seconds and provides 3 variations for each request.

What types of content can I create?

You can create social media posts for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, TikTok, and YouTube. You can also generate SEO-optimized blog articles ranging from 1000-5000 words with automatic keyword research, meta tags, and proper H1-H6 structure. Our AI content generator supports both short-form and long-form content creation.

Is there a limit to how much content I can generate?

Content limits depend on your subscription plan. Our Free plan includes 10 content generations per month. Starter plan offers 100 per month, Professional includes 500, and Enterprise provides unlimited content creation. All paid plans include unlimited AI-generated content variations and access to all features.

Can I edit the AI-generated content?

Absolutely! All generated content is fully editable. You can modify text, adjust tone, add or remove sections, and customize the content to match your brand voice. We provide a built-in editor with formatting options, or you can copy the content to your preferred editing tool.

How do I get content generator support if I need help?

We offer multiple support channels: browse our help center documentation, chat with our AI assistant 24/7, email our support team at support@runnwrite.com, or schedule a live demo call. Professional and Enterprise plans include priority support with dedicated account managers and faster response times.

Is the content SEO-optimized?

Yes! Our AI writing tool automatically includes SEO optimization for all blog content. This includes automatic keyword research, optimal keyword density (1-2%), meta title and description generation, proper header structure (H1-H6), internal linking suggestions, and readability optimization. Each article receives an SEO score with actionable improvement tips.
