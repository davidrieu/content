Troubleshooting

Solutions to common technical issues with content generator support

Why is my content generation taking longer than usual?

Long-form content (3000+ words) can take up to 2 minutes. If it's taking longer, check your internet connection and try refreshing the page. During peak hours, there may be slight delays. If issues persist, contact our AI content generator help team for assistance.

The AI generated content doesn't match my topic

Try being more specific in your topic description. Include context, key points you want covered, and your target audience. Our AI writing tool performs better with detailed inputs. You can also try regenerating or selecting a different variation from the 3 provided options.

I can't export my generated content

Ensure you have a paid plan - export features are not available on the free tier. Check that you've selected the export format (HTML, Markdown, PDF) before clicking export. If using ad blockers or privacy extensions, try disabling them temporarily as they may interfere with downloads.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and we'll send you a password reset link within minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it. The link expires after 24 hours, so request a new one if needed.

Best Practices

Tips to maximize results from our AI content generator

How can I get better AI-generated content?

Be specific and detailed in your inputs. Include target audience, tone, key points, and desired length. Review all 3 variations our AI writing tool provides. Use templates for consistency. Most importantly, always edit and personalize the content to add your unique perspective and brand voice.

Should I edit AI-generated content before publishing?

Yes! While our AI content generator produces high-quality content, we recommend reviewing and editing to ensure accuracy, add personal insights, include specific examples from your experience, and verify all facts. This makes the content uniquely yours and more valuable to your audience.

How often should I use AI-generated content?

Use our AI writing tool as often as you need! Many users generate daily social posts or weekly blog articles. The key is maintaining a consistent publishing schedule. Our AI content generator helps you maintain consistency without burnout, but always balance AI-created content with your original ideas.

What are the best keywords to use for SEO content?

Use our built-in keyword research tool to find relevant keywords with good search volume and low-medium difficulty. Focus on long-tail keywords (3-5 words) that match user intent. Our AI content generator automatically optimizes keyword density and placement for best SEO results.
